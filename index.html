Heat and darkness are separate on purpose. A book can be high-heat without being especially dark, or very dark without much on-page spice.

Anything we should avoid?

Avoid excludes a book. Okay means it can be included. No Preference leaves it neutral. Warning wording has been updated to feel clearer and more reader-centered.
Content note language was refined against the structure readers already see on The StoryGraph, which treats content warnings as a decision-making tool and distinguishes reader-flagged topics and severity. Future title-level enrichment can optionally add Minor, Moderate, and Graphic severity.
